## Publishing Principles

1. Do not hard-code prices in article prose. Use Sanity product references and `productPriceTable` blocks so price rows stay current.
2. Every article should link to at least one commercial hub, one related blog, and two product pages.
3. Publish content that answers the buyer's decision, not generic dry-fruit encyclopedia content.
4. Avoid direct competitor-branded SEO pages for now. Use competitor data as demand signal, not as a page title.
5. Use real Sanity product images where possible. Avoid AI-generated product visuals for product-led articles.
6. Keep health content practical and conservative: taste, use case, storage, satiety, snacking, and ingredients. Avoid unsupported treatment or disease claims.
